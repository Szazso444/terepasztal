Hills, rails and climbing trains

Every image is the game's own renderer. Click an image for full size.

  • In the game now: the tooltip shows "Elevation: level N". No metres anywhere; sea level is level 0.
  • In the game now: hill and mountain plateau tops are grass again, as in the locked version. The locked bank rule is kept as an option.
  • In the game now: rails follow the incline rules from your table. Each straight line has one continuous height profile, and pieces conform when neighbours are laid.
  • In the game now: track pieces on a climb are drawn as meshes bent to that profile, so they meet exactly at every joint.
  • In the game now: bridge platforms can stand on land. Straight track over them keeps the level of the higher bank.
  • In the game now: each locomotive and wagon body rests on its own front and rear bogies, so it follows the rail through the curves.
  • Preview only, your call: the slope grass tone, lighter higher levels, and snow. Soil skirts are removed.

3 · Height and snow

Higher levels paint lighter; snow by biome

Ground level stays exactly as it is. Each level above it is a few percent lighter. Snow starts as patches on the biome’s snow line level and turns to full snow one level up: 4 then 5 by default, 3 then 4 in taiga, 5 then 6 in desert. The tallest level is 4, so snow only shows on 4, and in taiga on 3 and 4. Desert never gets snow at the current heights. This map has only plains mountains, so the taiga images switch the hills in this view to taiga.

4 · Rail inclines

Your worked example, built in game

I shaped the terrain to your table and laid the line with the game's builder, with stone bridge platforms on tiles 12 to 14. Levels along the line:

1 1 1 2 2 2 1 2 2 1

Smooth tiles stay level. Tiles 6 and 7 are incline transitions that bend the rail up, tile 8 is a straight incline, and tiles 9 and 10 bend it level at 2. The bridge holds level 2 over the dip. Tiles 15 to 17 are decline transitions down to 0. On a monotonic climb the grade never exceeds one level per tile. The rules are written up in docs/rail-inclines.md.

5 · Trains on the line

Bodies ride their bogies

Each body sits on the line between its front and rear bogies, so it follows the rail through the bends. The sprites are still the placeholder art.
