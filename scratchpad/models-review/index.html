The 3D model the pipeline holds for each locomotive, next to the image it was made from. 39 of 39 are shown.

  • Model, same angle is the model seen as your image shows the engine; side, top and front end are the same model turned, at its real proportions (before the game squeezes its length).
  • Each model is made by ComfyUI from the one image, then the pipeline paints your image's own colours back onto it and mirrors the seen side onto the hidden one.
  • The 25 marked made today ran with default settings. The 14 made earlier are the pilot engines and the narrow batch.
