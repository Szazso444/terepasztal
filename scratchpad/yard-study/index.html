How should buildings meet the landscape?

Compare compact stone paving with a full forecourt. Both use the accepted station sprite at identical size and position, on the same continuous grass field. The proposed building allocation is 5 × 4 tiles; it is an art study, not a change to gameplay footprints.

A · Compact foundation

Stone close to the walls, a modest entrance apron, grass growing into the edges.

B · Full-footprint forecourt

The same stone extends across the lot, with a worn approach and irregular grassy margins.

The ochre reference figures represent 1.75 m adults against an approximately 2.1 m doorway. This is a provisional visual calibration; the whole asset library has not yet been rescaled. Terrain is sampled continuously in world coordinates, so texture variation and the stone/grass blend cross tile boundaries.

Direction to choose: A, B, or a mix—for example B at stations and industry, A at houses. Turn off the station to inspect the base and turn on the grid to inspect the footprint.
