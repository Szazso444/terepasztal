Terepasztal Slope Pitch

On a slope the game drew every train sprite as if it were a flat footprint lying on the ground, so the whole picture was squeezed or stretched along with the slope. At the steepest grade a train shrank to about 72 % of its height going up one way and grew to about 128 % coming down. The fix pitches each body along its own length and leaves its height alone.

  • Left is main as it is today, right is the same train at the same spot with the fix. The camera follows the train.
  • It applies to every train sprite: engines, tenders, wagons, bogies and loads, the game's own drawings and the pipeline's sprites alike. The Mk48 here is a pipeline sprite.
  • Rails only climb on straight track along the two tile axes, so these four directions are every case there is.

What it does and doesn't do
  • Bodies keep their height and their wheels stay on the rail under each bogie, as before.
  • A pitched body is sheared, not turned: its upright edges stay upright. With one drawing per direction that is the closest a sprite gets; a truly tilted engine would need extra sprites rendered nose-up and nose-down.
  • On the steepest grade the wheels on the near and far rail sit about 1 pixel off across the track. I could not see it in these renders.
  • Track, terrain and the rail profile are untouched; the change is two files in src/render plus a test.
