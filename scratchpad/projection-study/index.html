What the angles mean

View Camera elevation Ground edge angle Tile width : height

Azimuth is 45° in all three. Camera elevation and the angle of an edge on your screen are different quantities. The current game uses 64×32 tiles; it does not define a full 3D camera or a physical vertical unit. This study uses equal world units for building geometry.

The lower study matches the 105° / 105° / 150° dimetric axis separation in your reference. True isometric has three equal 120° separations.
