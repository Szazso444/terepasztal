Terepasztal Train Models 5

The fifth pilot: the rear views Codex generated (pull request 22), used on the five locomotives of the last pilot. Same poses, same sim as pilot 4; only the paint and the F7's trucks changed. The current look, pilot 4 and this one side by side.

  • Rear ends painted from the rear views. Each rear image's camera is found by fitting the model's outline to it (outline match 0.88 to 0.94), its colours are matched to the studio image, and it paints what the studio image cannot show: the F7's rear door and gangway, the SD40-2's long-hood end, the tenders' backs. Look at the Reversed scenes and the heading sheets' rear-facing half.
  • A fit that faced the wrong way, fixed. A box-like locomotive has nearly the same outline from either end, and three of five fits first put the rear image's camera at the front. A rear view is now searched only around the studio camera turned 180°.
  • F7 trucks from their own image. The truck Codex drew on its own was reconstructed separately, painted from that image, mirrored to both sides and put where the model's lumpy truck was, at the same size. The pilot, snowplow, coupler and steps still turn with it.
  • The SD40-2's truck image was rejected by Codex, so its trucks stay the model's own.

Bogies

Bogies are rendered per train, in the same run as the body. The F7's and SD40-2's come from their own reconstructions, mirrored to both sides, with their pilots, couplers and steps. The steam engines' leading bogies, trailing axle and pony truck are built round at the wheels measured on their images. Each sheet shows all 48 headings with the game's rail centres under it; current is the generic truck the game draws without them.
