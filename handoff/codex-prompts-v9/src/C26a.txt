repainted, never stretched, squeezed or sheared to fit: that is the author's standing rule (issue
#536), because the fit stretches whatever it is given and a stretched building looks wrong. The
refinery is a 1 x 1 works the game turns today by showing the one picture from every side. Three
things were learned on the coaling stage and the Fuel Pump and are built into this prompt: paint on
the guide, never on an earlier attempt of yours; the painter spreads a building 15 to 26 per cent
wider than its plinth unless it is told the width and checked (so the width is measured first); and
one correction at a time, replacing the last, never a growing pile.
