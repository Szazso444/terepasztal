THE MEASURING SCRIPT. Write assets/source/rotations-535/battery/measure-battery.mjs, your own. The shared assets/source/rotations-535/measure.mjs belongs to no job: other windows work on this branch at the same time and rewrote its numbers under each other, so do not use, edit or commit it. Your script reads a returned picture's alpha with pictureOf (tools/building-check.mjs; opaque means alpha above 128) and the view's scaffold json from ../scaffolds-535, and prints, per picture: the returned size and s0 = returned width / 1536; the silhouette numbers below; the opaque box; the base's width and k (THE SIZE); the two feet's slopes (rise over run; 0.50 for a 30 degree camera), reported and never refused; the height number below; the counts of opaque pixels with red, green and blue all at or above #ECE4D0 and within 12 (RGB distance) of #E8AA48; and PASS or FAIL for each machine test below. There is NO separate camera test, foot-ratio test or footprint test any more: the scaffold carries the camera and the plan, and the silhouette decides.

SILHOUETTE, the first test. The scaffold's silhouette is the polygon outline_polygon_px of the view's json (canvas px, 1536 x 1024), filled and scaled by s0 to the returned picture. The picture's silhouette is its opaque pixels with the enclosed holes filled (the polygon has none). Compute: (a) the overlap IoU (intersection over union of the two filled shapes) at s0 with no shift, reported; (b) the best fit: one uniform factor f between 0.85 and 1.15 on both axes and a shift (dx, dy), which is all Art's packing does to a picture (a uniform scale, then the base placed on the footprint), chosen to maximise the IoU (a coarse-to-fine search is fine); record f, dx, dy and the IoU at the best fit; (c) the boundary deviation at the best fit: the largest distance, in canvas px (divide pixels of the returned picture by s0 x f), from any point of the picture's outline to the scaffold's outline and from any point of the scaffold's outline to the picture's (the symmetric Hausdorff distance), after an opening of 2 canvas px on the picture's mask so that antialiasing specks do not count. PASS needs the IoU at the best fit of at least 0.90 AND a boundary deviation of at most 18 canvas px (3 game px; 6 canvas px make a game px on this canvas). A picture that fails is repainted, never nudged, stretched or reshaped to fit: the shape comes from the painting over the scaffold.
