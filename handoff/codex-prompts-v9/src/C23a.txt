1. THE BOUNDARY TEST CHANGES (Core's recalibration of 21 October 2026; it replaces every boundary test and every "boundary deviation at most 24 canvas px" below). The old test took the single largest distance between the two outlines (the symmetric Hausdorff distance), so one thin protrusion (a chimney pot, a post, a hose, a bargeboard tip) refused pictures that matched the scaffold everywhere else. Measure as before (the same best uniform fit, the same 2 canvas px opening of the picture's mask), then take, for the picture's outline against the scaffold's and for the scaffold's against the picture's, the whole set of point distances in canvas px. PASS needs: the IoU at the best fit at least 0.90 (unchanged) AND the 95th percentile at most 24 canvas px (3 game px; 8 canvas px make a game px) for each of the two sets (the larger of the two is the figure) AND the largest distance of both sets at most 48 canvas px (6 game px). Record IoU at s0, f, dx, dy, the IoU at the best fit, the maximum, and the two 95th percentiles. The percentile is the value at rank ceil(0.95 n) of the sorted distances. Every other test, and every identity detail the prompt names (a door, a window row, the high gable window), stays as written: a picture that passes the outline test and misses a named detail is still refused. MEASURE BEFORE YOU RECORD (after take, before set), in this order. There is NO separate camera test, foot-ratio test or footprint test any more: the scaffold carries the camera and the plan, and the silhouette decides.

- silhouette, the first test. The scaffold's silhouette is the polygon outline_polygon_px of the view's json (../scaffolds-535/quarry-r<v>.json: canvas px, 1024 x 1024), filled and scaled by s0 = returned width / 1024 to the returned picture. The picture's silhouette is its opaque pixels (alpha above 128) with the enclosed holes filled (the polygon has none). Compute (a) the overlap IoU (intersection over union of the two filled shapes) at s0 with no shift, reported; (b) the best fit: one uniform factor f between 0.85 and 1.15 on both axes and a shift (dx, dy), which is all Art's packing does to a picture, chosen to maximise the IoU (a coarse-to-fine search is fine); record f, dx, dy and the IoU at the best fit; (c) the boundary deviation at the best fit: the boundary distances, in canvas px (returned px divided by s0 x f): for every point of the picture's outline its distance to the scaffold's outline, and for every point of the scaffold's outline its distance to the picture's outline: two sets of distances; record the maximum of both (the old symmetric Hausdorff number) and the 95th percentile of each set (sort the distances, take the value at rank ceil(0.95 n)); the boundary figure is the larger of the two 95th percentiles (CORE REVIEW 1, rule 1), after an opening of 2 canvas px on the picture's mask so that antialiasing specks do not count.
