THE VIEWS. The tower is round, so a quarter turn changes only what stands on it: the windshaft
stub and its dark hub plate, and the door. The stub turns with the building (lower left, upper
left, upper right, lower right in r0 to r3). In r1 and r2 the front is away from the camera: the
stub and the plate stand BEHIND the tower, there is no door and no window on either visible face,
and the sail cross, which the game adds later, is seen from behind. The guides are drawn by the
game's own projection, canvas 1024 x 1024, 8 canvas px to a game px, anchor (the footprint's
centre at ground level) at (512, 832). The tower is the same in all four: foot ellipse centre
(512, 800), semi-axes 171.97 and 85.98 (ratio 0.50), cone tip at (512, 420). What differs:

  view  front is   plate centre (canvas)  hub from the anchor (game px)  stub leaves the cap
  r1    upper left  (384, 432)            (-16, -50)                     up and to the left, behind
  r2    upper right (640, 432)            ( 16, -50)                     up and to the right, behind
  r3    lower right (640, 560)            ( 16, -34)                     down and to the right, in front

The stub's top edge runs at slope 0.5 (one pixel up or down for two across) in every view. Only r3
has a door: on the lower-right face, centre (633.6, 817.6), 86.4 canvas px (10.8 game px, 2.1 m)
high. For r0 the numbers are C17c's: hub (-16, -34), door on the lower-left face.

MEASURE EVERY PICTURE BEFORE YOU GO ON. C17c wrote check-body.mjs in this folder; give it the view
(1, 2 or 3) and the table's hub and stub direction for it, and have it print each number below
with PASS or FAIL. Only the foot is read by machine:
- camera: from tools/building-fit.mjs, roundCamera(png) (ratio, and degrees off), and from
  tools/building-check.mjs, checkFile(path, 't1', 0, { round: true }), which must list no
  problem. Do not run it through the command line form: that writes
  assets/source/buildings-v2/report.json, which is not yours. The foot columns are those whose
  lowest pixel is within 0.4 of the building's width of the lowest pixel of all; W is their span,
  xc their middle, yc = lowest pixel - 0.25 W (the ellipse's centre), s = W / 42.99 picture px per
  game px.
Everything else you read off a zoomed crop and pass to the script as coordinates. A picture passes
when:
- foot ellipse: minor over major 0.50 +-0.04, at most 2 degrees off (a picture 2 to 3 degrees
  off that passes the rest is a near keeper: kept and reported; if the best attempt of the view is
  still more than 3 degrees off, stop that view, do not paint it again);
- the tower stands as the guide's: xc within 12 px of 512 and yc within 12 px of 800 (scale these
  by the returned width / 1024), and W within 3% of the W in hub.json of r0, so that the four views
  are one windmill at one scale;
- tower: (yc - the cone's tip) / W within 5% of 1.105 (1.05 to 1.16). The tip is the cone's own
  point, read on the crop: in r1 and r2 the plate stands higher than the tip, so the topmost
  opaque pixel is the plate, not the tip;
- stub: two points on the top edge of the stub, one near the cap or its edge, one near the plate,
  at least 40 px apart; the edge runs at slope 0.50 +-0.08 and in the view's direction (up to the
  left in r1, up to the right in r2, down to the right in r3);
- hub: the plate's centre, ((x - xc) / s, (y - yc) / s - 4), is within 2 game px of the view's
  hub: (-16, -50) in r1, (16, -50) in r2, (16, -34) in r3. The plate must be seen whole, as in the
  guide; in r1 and r2 nothing of the cap covers its middle;
- door: r3 only: its centre column is (x - xc) / W = +0.354 +-0.04; its height over W is 0.251
  +-0.03 (a 2.5 m door reads 0.30). In r1 and r2: no door, no window, nothing dark and
  door-shaped on the face;
- clean: pixels with hue 8 to 48 degrees, saturation 0.45 or more and value 0.60 or less (the sail
  timber; the stone's own warmth is not counted) outside the door, the stub and the hub plate: at
  most 25; opaque pixels (alpha above 128) with red, green and blue all at or above #ECE4D0: 0;
  within 12 (RGB distance) of selection amber #E8AA48: 0. A picture whose only miss is the
  near-white count is a keeper all the same: record the count, and Art caps those pixels when it
  packs the sprite; name it in a Correction only while another test also failed;
- by eye, on your own look at the zoomed picture: a round smooth tower, no sails or stocks or
  lattice, no plinth, no finial, nothing standing at the foot, nothing orange, the lower-left face
  lighter than the lower-right, the cap slate, and, in r1 and r2, the stub and plate behind the
  tower with nothing in front of the tower where a door would be.
