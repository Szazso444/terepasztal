THE MEASURING SCRIPT. Write assets/source/rotations-535/ironworks/measure-ironworks.mjs, your own. The shared assets/source/rotations-535/measure.mjs belongs to no job: other windows work on this branch at the same time and rewrote its numbers under each other, so do not use, edit or commit it. Your held width is the guide's number times (the returned picture's width / 1024) for every picture the tool returns, and k = held width / the measured base width (never the width of r0's picture, which is a reading, not a test). checkFile knows depots and one-tile buildings: for any other footprint its complaints about portals or the footprint are not a failure; the camera test is the two foot slopes within 0.03 of 0.50. Your script is built as follows. It reads a picture with pictureOf
(tools/building-check.mjs) and takes the walls' feet with measureBase (tools/building-fit.mjs); it
prints, per picture: the size; the opaque box (alpha above 128); the base corners w, s, e; the
lower-left and lower-right foot (s.x - w.x, e.x - s.x) and their ratio; the base's width e.x - w.x with its outline, and k; the slope of each foot (rise over run; a 30 degree camera gives 0.50) and its
distance from 0.50; the camera's error in degrees from checkFile(file, 't2x2', <view>) of
tools/building-check.mjs (never the command-line form: that writes report.json, which is not yours);
the counts of opaque pixels (alpha above 128) with red, green and blue all at or above #ECE4D0 and
within 12 (RGB distance) of #E8AA48; and PASS or FAIL for each test below that a machine can read.
Where measureBase puts a corner on a crane, a pipe, a canopy post or inside a quoin step, read the
outer corner of the wall's foot by eye on a zoomed crop and record both values.
