house is the game's most repeated building and is seen from one side only today. The pump-sized plan
fits a 1024 px canvas; the tall canvas is assumed to come back as asked (1024 x 1536); record what
it returns. Three things were learned on the coaling stage and the Fuel Pump and are built into this
prompt: paint on the guide, never on an earlier attempt of yours; the painter spreads a building 15
to 26 per cent wider than its plinth unless it is told the width and checked (so the width is
