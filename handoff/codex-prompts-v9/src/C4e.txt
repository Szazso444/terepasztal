MEASURE BEFORE YOU RECORD (after take, before set), in this order. There is NO separate camera test, foot-ratio test or footprint test any more: the scaffold carries the camera and the plan, and the silhouette decides.

- silhouette, the first test. The scaffold's silhouette is the polygon outline_polygon_px of the view's json (../scaffolds-535/fuel_stop-r<v>.json: canvas px, 1024 x 1024), filled and scaled by s0 = returned width / 1024 to the returned picture. The picture's silhouette is its opaque pixels (alpha above 128) with the enclosed holes filled (the polygon has none). Compute (a) the overlap IoU (intersection over union of the two filled shapes) at s0 with no shift, reported; (b) the best fit: one uniform factor f between 0.85 and 1.15 on both axes and a shift (dx, dy), which is all Art's packing does to a picture, chosen to maximise the IoU (a coarse-to-fine search is fine); record f, dx, dy and the IoU at the best fit; (c) the boundary deviation at the best fit: the largest distance, in canvas px (returned px divided by s0 x f), from any point of the picture's outline to the scaffold's outline and from any point of the scaffold's outline to the picture's (symmetric Hausdorff), after an opening of 2 canvas px on the picture's mask so that antialiasing specks do not count. PASS needs an IoU at the best fit of at least 0.90 AND a boundary deviation of at most 24 canvas px (3 game px; 8 canvas px make a game px). Write the small script yourself (it reads pictureOf from tools/building-check.mjs for the alpha; it is not committed unless the folder already has a measuring script for this family); a picture that fails is repainted, never nudged, stretched or reshaped to fit;

- width and scale: a measurement, never a refusal. The pier span is held at 516 px of the canvas (632 of 1254): on a crop, read the leftmost pier's foot and the rightmost pier's foot (outer edges; chute and chain do not count), and k = held span scaled by returned width / 1024, divided by that span. Record both; the picture goes on to the next tests whatever its width;

- node tools/building-fit.mjs <id>. Its base corners w, s and e give the lower-left foot s.x - w.x and the lower-right foot e.x - s.x: REPORTED, never refused (the slopes of the two feet, 0.50 for a 30 degree camera, go in the record too);

- height, as a proportion (scaling does not change it): (s.y - box.top) / (e.x - w.x), with the base corners s, e and w that node tools/building-fit.mjs prints for the picture as it was returned (the pier span e.x - w.x) and box.top its opaque box's top row (r0 reads 1.531: s.y 1128, box.top 154, span 636 of 1254; r2 reads 1.484). Read it on the picture and on the same view's bare scaffold render the same way (record it as scaffoldReference): within 15 per cent of the scaffold's; r0's number goes in the record too;

- light: the lower-left wall lighter than the lower-right wall, by eye on a crop;

- highlights (reported, NOT a pass rule: tools/atlas-cap.mjs, issue #465, caps both at packing; never refuse or stop a picture for them): count opaque pixels with all three channels at or above #ECE4D0, and within 12 (RGB distance) of #E8AA48, on your picture and on r0 (its fitted picture); Art caps the rest at packing;
