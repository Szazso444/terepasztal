- silhouette, the first test. The scaffold's silhouette is the polygon outline_polygon_px of the view's json (../scaffolds-535/fuel_pump-r<v>.json: canvas px, 1024 x 1024), filled and scaled by s0 = returned width / 1024 to the returned picture. The picture's silhouette is its opaque pixels (alpha above 128) with the enclosed holes filled (the polygon has none). Compute (a) the overlap IoU at s0 with no shift, reported; (b) the best fit: one uniform factor f between 0.85 and 1.15 on both axes and a shift (dx, dy), which is all Art's packing does to a picture, chosen to maximise the IoU (a coarse-to-fine search is fine); record f, dx, dy and the IoU at the best fit; (c) the boundary deviation at the best fit: the largest distance, in canvas px (returned px divided by s0 x f), from any point of the picture's outline to the scaffold's outline and from any point of the scaffold's outline to the picture's (symmetric Hausdorff), after an opening of 2 canvas px on the picture's mask. PASS needs an IoU at the best fit of at least 0.90 AND a boundary deviation of at most 24 canvas px (3 game px; 8 canvas px make a game px);
