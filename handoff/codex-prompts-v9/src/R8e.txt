THE BUILDING (r0). A red-brick pump house on cream limestone quoins and base. Its front is the long
side: two bays, the door bay (forest-green plank door, lamp over it, a small slate-roofed porch
gable a little forward of the wall), a downpipe, a plain bay, and a big quoined pier at the end. The
short side and the back are plain brick between quoins: no door, no window, no porch, no lamp. On
the house a dark steel frame with X bracing, and on it a riveted forest-green steel tank with two
oat-cream bands, a railing, a manhole and a vent, as long as the long side and as deep as the house,
covering the whole roof. At the end of the front next to the left-hand side wall stands a tall
forest-green hose crane: ONE post with one diagonal brace and ONE jib about 0.43 tile long (the
width of the short side), the hose hanging from the jib's end. Pipes with copper bands run from the
tank to the crane. THE LIGHT never turns with the building. It comes from the upper left: tops
lightest, the lower-left wall mid (r0's front, luma 112), the lower-right wall darkest (r0's short
side, 67). Camera: orthographic 2:1 isometric, 30 degrees down, no perspective; both wall feet fall
one pixel for every two across.
