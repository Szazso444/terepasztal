WHY THIS WAY. The game's camera is 30 degrees down, 2:1. A picture painted from another angle is
repainted, never stretched, squeezed or sheared to fit: that is the author's standing rule (issue
#536), because the fit stretches whatever it is given and a stretched building looks wrong. The
illustrated town hall has no turned views yet; the game turns it in quarter turns and today shows
the one picture from every side. Three things were learned on the coaling stage and the Fuel Pump
and are built into this prompt: paint on the guide, never on an earlier attempt of yours; the
painter spreads a building 15 to 26 per cent wider than its plinth unless it is told the width and
checked (so the width is measured first); and one correction at a time, replacing the last, never a
growing pile.

THE MEASURING SCRIPT. Write assets/source/rotations-535/measure.mjs once (if an earlier job of this
lane left it there, use it and fix only a wrong number). It reads a picture with pictureOf
(tools/building-check.mjs) and takes the walls' feet with measureBase (tools/building-fit.mjs); it
prints, per picture: the size; the opaque box (alpha above 128); the base corners w, s, e; the
lower-left and lower-right foot (s.x - w.x, e.x - s.x) and their ratio; the base's width e.x - w.x

the counts of opaque pixels (alpha above 128) with red, green and blue all at or above #ECE4D0 and
within 12 (RGB distance) of #E8AA48; and PASS or FAIL for each test below that a machine can read.
Where measureBase puts a corner on a crane, a pipe, a canopy post or inside a quoin step, read the
outer corner of the wall's foot by eye on a zoomed crop and record both values.
