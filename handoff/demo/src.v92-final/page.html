Codex art in the game

Every finished Codex picture laid into the approved tile tooltip map, painted the way the game paints its ground: its own swatches blended with soft, irregular borders, banks shaded, the game's trees, track and buildings on top. Version 2 answers the author's feedback of 12 October: waterfalls in the river's line, shores without tile cuts, relief and glacier options, one bridge, people on smooth paths, organic roads, raised wheat, one look for still water, working switches. Version 2.1 fixes what a check of it found: rivers open onto still water without banks, falls clear of trees, the game's relief by default, wandering glacier edges, roads that meander, Show me that frames every place. Version 3 answers the feedback of 12 October afternoon, and its fix round what a check of it found: the mountain’s flank is one slope painted as ground instead of stone walls (reliefs Game v2 and Smooth v2), waterfalls lie along their slope as wide as the river with white, broken spray off both sides over dark wet rock and moss, and a plunge pool of foam where they land, the glacier (an ice channel by default) ends in a rounded ice front with a near-black cave its melt runs out of, the plateau’s snow lies opaque with angular rocks poking through it, the town square is paved with small warm setts that the roads stop at, the wheat in front of the farm halt covers its foot, and river banks fade into the ground beside them. Version 8 answers the author’s notes of 14 October. Water: all of it moves now, the new default (Animated); Still brings back v7’s still water. Towns: hover over a town hall to see its town’s border, click to pin it, click again or press Escape to let it go; Show me Town borders goes to two small towns whose borders meet. Drag to pan, scroll or use + and − to zoom. Hover a tile for its tooltip, which names the Codex picture on it and its review verdict.

Version 9 answers the author’s notes of 16 October. Relief: only your two are left, Game v2 (the default) and Smooth v2; on the mountain’s right side, its faces turned to the lower right and the upper right, each level step now reads as a ledge (Game v2’s of its rock, Smooth v2’s a soft lip over a grassy riser), and the left side and everything off the mountain are as in v8. New is the wind: Weather (Breeze by default) and Wind from (W by default) move everything in it. The trees sway, gusts rolling over the forest; in the desert tumbleweeds roll and sand drifts; with Animated water, Waves (Swell by default, or Ripples and Chop) move the sea and the lakes and break on the shores the wind blows toward. Building life turns the windmill’s sails, lets the chimneys smoke and swings the quarry crane’s load. Coast: part of the peninsula west of the bay is a mangrove swamp. People: Fixed walk plants their feet (each pose stands where its foot came down, and the body steps on between poses), with Codex’s new back views; the pump hand’s back views (walking and carrying), the farmer’s back view carrying and the extra passengers 3 and 4 still slide a little until Codex repaints them. Building scale: True to people draws each building at the size its door and walls ask beside a 1.75 m person. Sleepers: Even spaces the narrow line’s sleepers evenly across its joins and onto the bridge. Version 9.1: the open water is gentler (Wave strength, Gentle by default, or v9 for v9’s), the windmill’s sails follow the wind and its gusts and turn in the plane of the face the cap points along, and a fifth as many dead trees stand in the desert. Version 9.2: chimney smoke climbs two to three storeys and is sparser (Smoke, Tall or v9.1), and Building scale adds Smaller (the default, each building at the size a 1.75 m person asks) and People larger, with Figures standing a person at each door; Road bridges (On by default): a road may cross water on a bridge of its own and people cross on it, one over the creek by the farm and one over the river by the pump, right beside the narrow line’s bridge, so the two are one wider bridge, the rail on one half and the road on the other; and a Storm brings more weather (Storm effects, On by default: an overcast, rain slanted by the wind, rings on the water, a darker, wetter ground and lightning now and then) while Fog is a switch of its own (Off, Valley mist or Thick).

To compare with v8, pick each new switch’s v8 setting: Weather Calm, Waves v8, Building life Off, Coast Forest, People v8, Building scale As before, Sleepers v8; the scene is then v8’s but for the mountain’s right side. Road bridges Off, Storm effects Off and Fog Off are v9.1’s scene. Placeholders until Codex delivers them: the mangroves and the weeping willows (#460), and the windmill’s sails, which the demo cuts out of its picture to turn them (#461). In the side panel, Show me goes to each new place.
