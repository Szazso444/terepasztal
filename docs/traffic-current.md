# Train traffic control: current behaviour

How the simulation decides that a train must slow or stop for another train and how it resolves contention at junctions, as implemented in `src/sim/traffic.ts`, `src/sim/fleet.ts` and `src/sim/trains.ts`. Descriptive only; no changes are proposed.

## 1. Overview

`Fleet.tick` (`src/sim/fleet.ts:576`) runs in this order: rebuild the tile occupancy map from car poses (`src/sim/fleet.ts:578`, `src/sim/fleet.ts:278-289`); rebuild per-tile "reservations" for the next 8 tiles of every moving train's path, first come first served (`src/sim/fleet.ts:579`, `src/sim/fleet.ts:322-329`); `Traffic.assign` recomputes claims and every train's `claimLimit`/`claimBlocker` (`src/sim/fleet.ts:580`, `src/sim/traffic.ts:166`); every train ticks (`src/sim/fleet.ts:582-585`); `Traffic.observe` does blocked/stuck bookkeeping (`src/sim/fleet.ts:586`); proactive parking of trains held on their blocker's path (`src/sim/fleet.ts:587-602`); jam-chain resolution (`src/sim/fleet.ts:603-670`). Claims are computed from the previous tick's positions and paths and consumed by `tickMove` in the same tick.

## 2. Track sections

`rebuildSections` (`src/sim/traffic.ts:81-126`) runs whenever `track.version` changes, which also clears every claim (`src/sim/traffic.ts:167-170`). A tile is a node when it has no track, has more than one link (switches and crossings both have two, `src/world/track.ts:20-36`), is a station gate tile with track ("platform", `src/sim/traffic.ts:85-87`) or has fewer than two connected edges (dead end) (`src/sim/traffic.ts:88-95`). Nodes get the singleton id `-(key+1)` (`src/sim/traffic.ts:101`); plain tiles are flood-filled along `track.connected` edges into numbered sections (`src/sim/traffic.ts:104-125`), kept in `sections` (tile→id) and `sectionTiles` (id→tiles) (`src/sim/traffic.ts:61-62`). `sectionOf` falls back to the singleton id (`src/sim/traffic.ts:132-134`); `sectionLength` (`src/sim/traffic.ts:136-139`) has no callers. `HORIZON = 6` tiles (`src/sim/traffic.ts:7`) bounds claim extent (§3).

## 3. Claims

`claims` maps tile key → train id (`src/sim/traffic.ts:60`).

**Release.** Each tick a keep-set per train is built from its car tiles (poses rounded with `Math.floor(p+0.5)`, `src/sim/traffic.ts:143-150`) plus, only while `state === 'moving'`, its whole `pathAhead()` (`src/sim/traffic.ts:174-179`); every claim not in its owner's keep-set, or owned by a removed train, is deleted (`src/sim/traffic.ts:180-183`). Hence a train that stops moving (arrival sets `loading`/`waiting`, `src/sim/trains.ts:1267-1270`; also `idle`, `yielding`, `noFuel`) keeps only the tiles under its cars; a retreating train keeps only tiles that were already claimed and lie on its new hold path. Platform slots are a per-level count (`src/sim/stations.ts:183-185`, `src/sim/stations.ts:243-245`), independent of tile claims.

**Order.** Trains are processed moving-first, then ascending `yieldCount`, then descending weight, then ascending id (`src/sim/traffic.ts:184-190`). Car tiles are always claimed, overwriting other holders; if the holder's cars are also there an overlap episode is logged (`src/sim/traffic.ts:192-200`).

**Extent.** `claimLimit`/`claimBlocker` reset to `Infinity`/`null` (`src/sim/traffic.ts:201-202`); trains not moving or `holding` claim nothing ahead (`src/sim/traffic.ts:203`). Otherwise the walk covers the full remaining path (`pathAhead()`, `src/sim/trains.ts:855-868`, one entry per tile with its arc). At a section change it stops when the tile is more than `HORIZON` of arc ahead and the previous tile was a node (`sectionStart < 0`) (`src/sim/traffic.ts:214-219`). Since a plain section always ends in a node, the result is: at least 6 tiles of arc, then to the end of the plain section being traversed plus its bounding node tile.

**Contention while walking** (`src/sim/traffic.ts:220-249`). A tile held by another train that is moving with heading dot product > 0.1 (`headingOf`, `src/sim/traffic.ts:152-159`) is same-direction following: `claimLimit` = distance to that tile, nothing rolled back, break (`src/sim/traffic.ts:228-233`). Any other holder (oncoming or standing) triggers the whole-section rule: if the tile is plain, tiles of that section were added, and the train's rounded head tile (`Math.floor(x+0.5)`) is not itself in that section (`headIn`, `src/sim/traffic.ts:235-237`), all tiles added for the section are unclaimed except those under the train's own cars, and `claimLimit` becomes the arc of the section's entry tile (`src/sim/traffic.ts:238-244`). If the blocked tile is a node, or the head is already inside the section, the claim stops just short of the blocked tile (`src/sim/traffic.ts:245`). `counters.waits` increments here once per train per tick (`src/sim/traffic.ts:247`).

## 4. Slowing and stopping

`tickMove` (`src/sim/trains.ts:1050`) computes `blockDist`. It scans path points from `pathPos+0.3` to `pathPos+LOOKAHEAD` (2.5 tiles, `src/sim/trains.ts:123`, `src/sim/trains.ts:1056-1057`), skipping its own car tiles (`src/sim/trains.ts:1060-1068`), and stops at the first tile in `ctx.occupied`: `blockDist = arc − pathPos − HOLD_GAP` (0.45, `src/sim/trains.ts:125`, `src/sim/trains.ts:1069-1072`). Unless `holding`, the claim limit is applied the same way when smaller: `blockDist = claimLimit − HOLD_GAP`, `blocker = claimBlocker` (`src/sim/trains.ts:1077-1080`). `blocked` is true only when `blockDist < 0.3` (`src/sim/trains.ts:1081-1082`).

**Speed.** `vmax` combines engine speed, load, path factor (curves, bridges, hills), weather, biome, eco mode and reversing (`src/sim/trains.ts:1148-1155`). `vStop = sqrt(2·DECEL·min(remaining, blockDist))` (`src/sim/trains.ts:1156`); the target is the smaller, approached at `ACCEL = 0.7` or `DECEL·1.5` (`src/sim/trains.ts:119-120`, `src/sim/trains.ts:1157-1159`). So there is a braking curve toward the hold point, and the step is additionally hard-clamped to `blockDist` (`src/sim/trains.ts:1160-1165`), so no overrun is possible; a floor of `0.02·gdt` keeps trains creeping whenever `blockDist > 0`. Occupancy is seen only 2.5 tiles ahead; a claim limit is seen from any distance.

**While blocked** `blockedTime` accumulates; after `REROUTE_AFTER = 6 s` one reroute avoiding occupied (not claimed) tiles is tried, repeated every `RETRY_EVERY = 20 s` (`src/sim/trains.ts:127-129`, `src/sim/trains.ts:1119-1144`). Dynamic trains additionally probe every 4 s for an oncoming train that reserved the next 8 tiles and, if found, try a forward-only detour avoiding its reserved tiles (`src/sim/trains.ts:1083-1118`).

**Other stops.** Fuel or power failure sets `noFuel`/`noPower`, speed 0 (`src/sim/trains.ts:497-508`, `src/sim/trains.ts:1168-1174`), retried every 3 s (`src/sim/trains.ts:965-976`); a tank under 30 % gives eco mode at 0.6 speed (`src/sim/trains.ts:131-133`, `src/sim/trains.ts:1145`). At stations `waiting` lasts until a platform slot frees (`src/sim/trains.ts:919-925`); dwell is bounded by `MIN_DWELL 2`, `MAX_DWELL 40`, `MAX_DWELL_FULL 240` and the `waitFull` rule (`src/sim/trains.ts:121`, `src/sim/trains.ts:134-136`, `src/sim/trains.ts:1442-1450`); `idle` departs after 10 s (`src/sim/trains.ts:926-928`).

## 5. Junction contention

Switches and crossings carry no state; the path chooses the exit (`src/world/pathfinding.ts:45`, `src/world/track.ts:101-110`). A junction is one node tile, so two paths across it are serialised by the tile claim: the first claimant in the §3 sort gets it and the other stops `HOLD_GAP` short (or at its section entry if a section lies between). No other arbitration of junction priority exists.

Contention is untangled by `Fleet.tick`:

1. **Proactive parking** (`src/sim/fleet.ts:587-602`): a moving, non-holding train with `claimLimit ≤ 1.5` whose cars stand on its blocker's `pathTileKeys` (cars + remaining path + wanted path, `src/sim/trains.ts:1612-1624`) is marked blocked and calls `retreat`, provided `yieldUntil` has passed; success logs a yield.
2. **Chain resolution** (`src/sim/fleet.ts:603-670`): trains blocked for `MUTUAL_GRACE = 4 s` (`src/sim/fleet.ts:679`) start a chain along `blockedBy` (≤ 8 links, loops detected, `src/sim/fleet.ts:612-625`). An `idle`/`waiting` tail with someone behind it moves first: `retreat`, else `parkElsewhere` (`src/sim/fleet.ts:629-642`). Otherwise the chain is dead when it loops, its tail is itself blocked past grace, or its tail is in a non-moving, non-transient state (`src/sim/fleet.ts:645-650`); the chain sorted by ascending `yieldCount`, ascending weight, descending id (`src/sim/fleet.ts:654-656`) is asked to `retreat` in turn and the first success wins, one per chain per tick (`src/sim/fleet.ts:657-663`). If nobody could and the chain loops, `Traffic.deadlock` is reported in the single tick where the head's `blockedTime` crosses 16 s (`src/sim/fleet.ts:664-669`), deduplicated per chain signature for 60 s (`src/sim/traffic.ts:316-337`).

`retreat` (`src/sim/trains.ts:1529-1610`) returns false immediately when no car is on the other train's path ("already clear", `src/sim/trains.ts:1553-1554`). A hold tile must be off their path, unoccupied, not claimed by anyone else (`ctx.claimedBy`) and not a switch/crossing (`src/sim/trains.ts:1545-1551`). It paths forward to the nearest hold tile (cost ≤ 600), else backwards with a consist flip (`src/sim/trains.ts:1556-1565`), then tries to go deeper, `ceil(ownLen)` tiles down to 1 (`src/sim/trains.ts:1569-1582`), and fails if the rear cars would still stand on their path (`src/sim/trains.ts:1584-1586`). Success sets `holding`, `yieldCount++`, `yieldUntil = now+30`, state `moving` (`src/sim/trains.ts:1600-1609`). At the end of the hold path the train enters `yielding` (`src/sim/trains.ts:1216-1219`) and every 2 s tries to dispatch avoiding occupied tiles, or any path once 60 s past `yieldUntil` (`src/sim/trains.ts:929-950`); meanwhile `noteBlockerOnPlainPath` keeps `blockedBy` on the first occupant of its wanted path so chain resolution still sees it (`src/sim/trains.ts:997-1023`). A holding train ignores `claimLimit` (`src/sim/trains.ts:1077`) and claims nothing ahead (`src/sim/traffic.ts:203`): it moves on occupancy alone.

`parkElsewhere` (`src/sim/fleet.ts:535-562`) sends an idle/waiting tail to the nearest other station with a free slot and a platform tile that is off the waiting train's path and unoccupied.

## 6. Stuck detection and statistics

`observe` (`src/sim/traffic.ts:339-387`): `blocked` adds `gdt` to blocked seconds (`src/sim/traffic.ts:346`); head movement over `PROGRESS_EPS = 0.05` resets the progress clock (`src/sim/traffic.ts:349-352`); a train that wants to move (`moving`/`yielding`/`waiting`, `src/sim/traffic.ts:348`) with no movement for `STUCK_AFTER = 30 s` (`src/sim/traffic.ts:9`) opens a stuck episode (`src/sim/traffic.ts:369-384`), closed by a move over 0.5 tiles (`src/sim/traffic.ts:353-365`) or by ceasing to want to move (`src/sim/traffic.ts:366-368`). Overlaps are deduplicated per pair for 5 s (`src/sim/traffic.ts:281-287`). Episodes are capped at 400 (`src/sim/traffic.ts:275-280`); `verbose` prints them (`src/sim/traffic.ts:58`, `src/sim/traffic.ts:278`); `onEpisode` is a hook nothing sets. `stuckTrains()` (`src/sim/traffic.ts:389-394`) feeds `Notices.refresh` every 0.5 s (`src/game.ts:1881-1889`), which raises a `bad` stuck notice or an `info` held notice after 8 s blocked (`src/sim/notices.ts:62-70`); the advisor tips past 20 s (`src/game.ts:1662-1663`). The debug panel row shows only the counters (`src/game.ts:2285-2289`); `report()` (`src/sim/traffic.ts:396-416`, last 60 episodes) is console-only. `src/sim/stats.ts` holds resource flows, no traffic data.

## 7. Interaction with dynamic routing

`badTargets` (`src/sim/trains.ts:383-387`) expire after 60 s when a station gave nothing and would be picked again (`src/sim/trains.ts:1469-1473`), and after 240 s when no path exists at departure (`src/sim/trains.ts:1515-1521`) or after 20 s of yielding with no blocker (`src/sim/trains.ts:955-961`). `dynamicTargets` collects the targets of other dynamic trains in `moving`/`yielding`/`waiting` (`src/sim/fleet.ts:331-342`); `chooseNext` skips those stations for pick-ups (`src/sim/fleet.ts:449`, `src/sim/fleet.ts:474`) but not for drops or contract destinations (`src/sim/fleet.ts:414-434`, `src/sim/fleet.ts:470`). Neither `chooseNext` nor `dispatch` consults claims; `avoid` predicates exclude only occupied tiles (`src/sim/trains.ts:1507-1512`, `src/sim/trains.ts:934`, `src/sim/trains.ts:1124`) or, for the anticipate detour, reserved tiles (`src/sim/trains.ts:1103-1104`). Pathfinding is Dijkstra over (tile, entry edge) with a 0.2 penalty on a switch's curved leg (`src/world/pathfinding.ts:17-61`).

## 8. Known limits

- No lineside signals or aspects; a train learns of conflicts only through its own path walk in `assign`.
- No headway or block occupancy beyond the whole-section rule; same-direction spacing relies on the 2.5-tile occupancy scan (`src/sim/trains.ts:123`).
- Claims are recomputed every tick from a priority sort; a section can be rolled back the tick after it was granted (`src/sim/traffic.ts:238-244`).
- Junction priority is not configurable: it is the sort at `src/sim/traffic.ts:184-190`; chain resolution uses the opposite weight order (`src/sim/fleet.ts:654-656`).
- Routing never avoids claimed track, only occupied tiles, so reroutes can lead straight into a held section.
- Braking is a per-tick curve with a hard clamp, not a signalled braking distance; occupancy lookahead ignores speed.
- `counters.waits` (debug "holds") counts ticks, not events (`src/sim/traffic.ts:247`); `headOn` is declared (`src/sim/traffic.ts:13`, `src/sim/traffic.ts:66`) but never raised.
