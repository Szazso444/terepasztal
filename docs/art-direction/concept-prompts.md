# Concept image prompts

Built-in image-generation tool. These prompts create visual references, not runtime assets.
The implementation guide's geometry, scale and functionality rules take precedence over
incidental details in the pictures. The game keeps its procedural asset pipeline.

## 01 — Land & Biomes

Create landscape visual implementation reference board titled '01 — LAND & BIOMES' for a pastoral railway strategy game. Warm parchment editorial sheet, crisp consistent pixel-art-inspired stepped shading, 2:1 orthographic isometric tiles, upper-left light, muted forest greens, limestone, teal water and warm earth. No perspective horizon or cinematic blur. Top two-thirds: six equal clearly separated isometric square 4x4-tile patches with labels 'PLAINS', 'FOREST', 'DESERT', 'TAIGA', 'SWAMP', 'OCEAN'. Plains: low moss grass, sparse tiny flowers, golden wheat patch. Forest: dark leaf-litter ground and rounded oak/birch canopy groups with open spaces. Desert: pale ochre sand, sandstone, sparse cactus and palm by an oasis. Taiga: blue-green spruce, pale ground, small restrained snow patches. Swamp: dark peat, shallow teal pools, reeds and deadwood. Ocean: calm teal water with sandy and rocky shoreline and a small island. Every patch is same scale and each includes a short clear railway segment to demonstrate readable contrast against the ground. Lower third: enlarged orderly diamond tile studies labeled 'GRASS', 'SAND', 'ROCK', 'HILL', 'MOUNTAIN', 'SHORE', 'CITY'; city tile neutral pale paving with narrow road, no giant building. Include adjoining grass-to-sand and water-to-shore pairs with naturally matching edges, no dark grid border around each ground tile. Terrain quiet with large colour clusters, no speckled noisy texture, no flowers everywhere. Water has restrained horizontal highlights; cliffs shaded solid faces. Compact footer 'Concept reference • preserve tile geometry and shared lighting'. This is an art direction board, not exact executable terrain masks. Coherent with handcrafted evergreen-and-ivory railway world, practical readable asset studies rather than scenic painting.

## 02 — Nature & Small Objects

Use case stylized-concept. Create landscape game asset art direction sheet title '02 — NATURE & SMALL OBJECTS'. Cohesive handcrafted pastoral railway pixel-art-inspired look, warm pale parchment background, 2:1 isometric orthographic object studies with crisp stepped shading, upper-left light, chunky coherent colour clusters, no smooth painted sections, no photorealism, no blur. Readable generous grid with exactly 15 labeled studies arranged 5 columns by 3 rows. Row one labels 'ROUND TREE', 'OAK', 'BIRCH', 'PINE', 'SPRUCE'. Each contains two variations of that tree: round simple broadleaf canopy; oak broad heavy branching; birch slender white trunks; pine sparse tiered canopy; spruce dense tapered silhouette. Row two labels 'PALM', 'CACTUS', 'DEAD TREE', 'BUSH', 'REEDS'. Row three labels 'FLOWERS', 'ROCKS', 'BOULDERS', 'COAL SEAM', 'OIL SEEP'. Flowers four tiny muted wildflower clusters, not giant flowers; rocks faceted low pale grey; boulders taller weathered angular masses; coal seam dark layered exposed rock with subtle broken highlight; oil seep very small dark muted oily ground patch without huge rainbow neon sheen. Nature should frame railways not overwhelm them. Small bottom strip shows one rail segment with trees set back from sleepers, small shadows attached to trunk bases and ground contact. Bottom text 'Vary silhouette and placement. Keep rail corridors clear.' Include tiny human silhouette as scale comparison beside the trees. Objects isolated with restrained contact shadows, no square soil pedestals under individual plants, no fantasy mushrooms or giant crystals. These are concept studies to translate into procedural sprite families.
