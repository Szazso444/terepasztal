SIZE AND CAMERA. In the game a person is about 9 px tall on a 64x32 px tile. At that size a
profession reads by the hat, the clothes and the load; tools read at zoom 2 to 4. That is
accepted. The camera is orthographic 2:1 isometric, 30 degrees down; proportions are adult and
realistic, 7 to 7.5 heads (not chibi). Two views:
- FRONT: walking toward the lower right of the screen (tile +x). We see the face and the
  front-right side in 3/4.
- BACK: walking toward the upper right of the screen (tile -y). We see the back and the right side
  in 3/4; the face and front clothing are hidden.
In both views the right leg is the near one.

FOUR DISTINCT POSES, in place, following the guide of each frame:
- f0 right foot forward: the near (right) leg reaches forward, heel down; the far leg trails, toe
  on the ground; the left arm swings forward.
- f1 passing: the right foot is planted under the hips; the left knee is bent, its foot lifted.
- f2 left foot forward: the far (left) leg reaches forward and the near leg trails, so the thighs
  cross the other way from f0; the right arm swings forward.
- f3 passing: the left foot is planted and the right foot lifted.
"Forward" is toward the upper right in the back view and toward the lower right in the front view.
The head bobs at most 2% of the height. Same person, clothes, load and size in every frame.

MEASURE BEFORE ACCEPTING (update check-poses.mjs). Let s = returned width / 1024. This block adapts
the 5% box rule. For a walking figure the height and the feet are tested. The width is only
recorded, because swinging arms and a load make a figure wider than the mannequin.
- If the return is not 2:3, record it as a failed attempt and paint again.
- Height: the figure from the top of the hat to the lowest sole (alpha above 128; leave out a
  load that rises above the hat) must be within 5% of 1152 * s. Retry otherwise.
- Feet: record how far the lowest sole lies from y = 1344 * s. Do not retry for it; the assembly
  below stands each pose on its own soles.
- Width: record it. Retry only when the figure, without its load, is more than 25% wider than the
  guide's box. That means another build or a second figure.
- The cycle: lay f0 over f2 at 50% opacity. The thighs must cross in an X, not lie on top of each
  other. In f1 and f3 the lifted foot must be the other one. Retry a pose that repeats another or
  has the wrong leg forward.
- The person: same face, skin, hair, clothes and load as the identity reference; no outline round
  the whole figure; water teal, not cyan.
