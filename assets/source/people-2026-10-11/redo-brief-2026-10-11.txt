SIZE AND CAMERA. In the game a person is about 9 px tall on a 64x32 px tile. At that size a
profession reads by the hat, the clothes and the load; tools read at zoom 2 to 4. That is
accepted. The camera is orthographic 2:1 isometric, 30 degrees down; proportions are adult and
realistic, 7 to 7.5 heads (not chibi). Two views:
- FRONT: walking toward the lower right of the screen (tile +x). We see the face and the
  front-right side in 3/4.
- BACK: walking toward the upper right of the screen (tile -y). We see the back and the right side
  in 3/4; the face and front clothing are hidden.
In both views the right leg is the near one.

FOUR DISTINCT POSES, in place:
- f0 right foot forward: the near (right) leg reaches forward, heel down; the far leg trails, toe
  on the ground; the left arm swings forward.
- f1 passing: the right foot is planted under the hips; the left knee is bent, its foot lifted
  beside the right shin.
- f2 left foot forward: the far (left) leg reaches forward and the near leg trails, so the thighs
  cross the other way from f0; the right arm swings forward.
- f3 passing: the left foot is planted and the right foot lifted.
"Forward" is toward the upper right in the back view and toward the lower right in the front view.
The head bobs at most 2% of the height. Same person, clothes, load and size in every frame.

GEOMETRY OF A POSE (exact). Each pose is one picture: canvas 1024x1536 (portrait), transparent, one
figure.
- The guide's figure stands centred: the soles' line at y = 1344, the top of the hat at y = 192
  (1152 px, 75% of the height), the hips at x = 512.
- A load on one shoulder may reach further to that side; the body stays centred.

THE STRIP. When a strip's four poses are accepted, build <strip>.png from them with
assemble-strips.mjs (Node, pngjs only; write it). Do not use export-*.cjs: they need sharp, and
they squeezed the carries and centred each silhouette instead of the body.
- Scale every pose of the strip by one factor, the same in x and y: 360 / (1152 * returned
  width / 1024). The figure is then 360 px tall, as in the other strips. Use an area-averaging
  downscale.
- Place each pose so that its guide point (512,1344), times returned width / 1024, lands on the
  frame's anchor.
- Walk strips: 768x512, four 192 px columns, anchors (96,480), (288,480), (480,480), (672,480),
  as in README.md.
- Carry strips: 1024x512, four 256 px columns, anchors (128,480), (384,480), (640,480),
  (896,480).
- The poses are anchored at the hips, so a load on one side reaches further than half the
  frame's width. Measure from the anchor: if any walk frame reaches more than 88 px to either
  side of its anchor, build that strip with 256 px columns too. If any frame in 256 px columns
  (a carry strip, or a walk strip so widened) reaches more than 120 px to either side, use 320 px
  columns (1280x512, anchors 160 + 320k).
- Never scale x and y separately. Never clip a load.
Record the canvas, the columns, the anchors and the scale in prompts.json.

CANVAS SIZE. Ask for 1024x1536 px for every pose. Keep each picture exactly as returned, and record
its real size as "returned": [w, h].

MEASURE BEFORE COMMITTING. Write check-poses.mjs in this folder (Node, pngjs only). Let
s = returned width / 1024.
- If the return is not 2:3, do not measure it against the scaled guide: record it as an attempt
  that failed the box test and paint again.
- For each pose, compare the painted box (the pixels with alpha above 128) with the guide's box,
  scaled by s. Retry when its width or height is more than 5% off, or when its centre is further
  from the guide box's centre than 5% of the guide box's width.
- Then lay f0 over f2 at 50% opacity. The legs must cross the other way, forming an X, not lie on
  top of each other. In f1 and f3 the lifted foot must be the other one.
- Retry a pose that repeats another.
Make at most three new attempts per pose. If a pose still repeats another after three, keep the
best, record it, and say so in your message: Core can fall back to a 0, 1, 0, 1 cycle. Check the
first strip (pump-hand-back-walk) fully before you go on. Before committing a strip, scale it down
so the figure is 9 px tall and play its four frames in order. It must walk.
