SILHOUETTE, the first test. The scaffold's silhouette is the polygon outline_polygon_px of the view's json (canvas px, 1024 x 1024), filled and scaled by s0 to the returned picture. The picture's silhouette is its opaque pixels with the enclosed holes filled (the polygon has none). Compute: (a) the overlap IoU (intersection over union of the two filled shapes) at s0 with no shift, reported; (b) the best fit: one uniform factor f between 0.85 and 1.15 on both axes and a shift (dx, dy), which is all Art's packing does to a picture (a uniform scale, then the base placed on the footprint), chosen to maximise the IoU (a coarse-to-fine search is fine); record f, dx, dy and the IoU at the best fit; (c) the boundary deviation at the best fit: the boundary distances, in canvas px (divide pixels of the returned picture by s0 x f): for every point of the picture's outline its distance to the scaffold's outline, and for every point of the scaffold's outline its distance to the picture's outline: two sets of distances; record the maximum of both (the old symmetric Hausdorff number) and the 95th percentile of each set (sort the distances, take the value at rank ceil(0.95 n)); the boundary figure is the larger of the two 95th percentiles (CORE REVIEW 1, rule 1), after an opening of 2 canvas px on the picture's mask so that antialiasing specks do not count. PASS needs the IoU at the best fit of at least 0.90 AND a boundary deviation with the 95th percentile at most 24 canvas px AND the maximum at most 48 canvas px (3 game px; 8 canvas px make a game px on this canvas). A picture that fails is repainted, never nudged, stretched or reshaped to fit: the shape comes from the painting over the scaffold.
